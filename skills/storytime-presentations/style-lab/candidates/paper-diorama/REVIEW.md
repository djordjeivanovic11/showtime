# Paper Diorama — self-review

Judged on the final renders in `renders/` (5 states + 4 midframes) and a live
keyboard run (advance, back, notes overlay, reduced motion; no page errors).

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| First-glance story clarity | 5 | Ring + two lettered buses + one gauge; the gap change is readable without the narration, and the loop labels say the mechanism outright. |
| World or template? | 5 | Every state is a lit shadow box with layered cut paper, grain, string and pins; nothing resembles a slide layout, and the numbers stand in the landscape. |
| Character warmth / usefulness | 4 | Pip points, checks his watch, throws his arms up and grins; the poses match the beats. He stays at the edge and never interacts physically with a bus. |
| Scaffold persistence | 5 | Route, stops, both buses, gauge and Pip are single DOM objects tweened between states; buses travel along the ring by time. |
| Legibility at distance | 4 | Tag 50 px, gauge numbers 46 px, labels 28–32 px, small print 24 px, all on cream cards. Stop numbers (30 px) and bus letters (about 29 px at the back) are at the floor. |
| Polish / craft | 4 | Shadows per plane, jittered edges, ink-eroded stamp and a swing ease look handmade. Some areas are thin: the meadow is a large flat green, the sidewalk is a plain slab, and the riders are simple. |
| Setting fit | 5 | Education, nonprofit, public science and family audiences: warm, unintimidating, honest small print kept. |
| One-shot reliability | 3 | The kit makes the pieces easy, but layout collisions (strings, reserved corners, props hiding numbers, plane peeking) needed four render rounds to find. A model must render and look. |
| PPTX feasibility | 3 | Planes, buses, Pip and the gauge map cleanly to Morph-named pictures and native shapes. Ring travel, pop-ups, swing and grain need approximations or intermediate slides. |

## Three weakest things in the final renders

1. **The tabletop meadow is a lot of flat green** (states 1–3), especially the
   bottom-left and right gutters. With more time: a few paper hedges, a pond
   or a second row of tiny houses on the meadow's rim, kept clear of the ring
   and corners. The paper layering also needs a visible edge thickness so it
   reads more as a diorama and less as a flat map.
2. **"HOLD THE EARLY BUS" is told, not shown.** Bus B parks with its door open
   and a tag, but nothing shows the holding or the spacing being restored.
   With more time: a small teal paper hand-signal on the pole, plus a faint
   dotted spacing arc between the held B and the distant A. The bookend would
   then visibly resolve the opening bunch.
3. **The reveal's background is busy behind "5.0".** The stakes and
   "gaps 10 + 10" card sit on top of the far-hill houses. With more time: a
   cleared patch of hill (or the houses moved right) behind the numerals, and
   a gentle hill-shadow under the stakes so they read as planted, not floating.

Also noted: the three opening buses are small (letters about 22 px). That is fine for an
establishing shot, but the bunching could be more dramatic with the buses nearer
and larger, cresting the hill toward Pip.
