## Three weakest things in the final renders
1. **The rider is small and far right in the bookend (state 4).** The character's "resolved" moment is a ~230 px figure pointing up, and the emotional payoff is weaker than in the pixel reference. *With more time:* give the reveal its own framing in which the rider stands at the kerb in the foreground, larger and facing the lightbox, with the bus layout moved so they do not overlap.
2. **The street props are less crafted than the map.** The buses are plain flat elevations (tiny `LOOP` blinds, no interior life), and the skyline is a regular grid of windows. *With more time:* add a passenger silhouette row in the windows of the bunched buses (full, full, empty: the bunching story told in the opening shot), vary the building rhythm, and add a faint wet-road reflection of the lightbox.
3. **The held bus in state 4 is cropped at the left edge**, and the HOLD plate sits slightly above it, so the link between "this bus" and "hold" relies on proximity. *With more time:* reframe so the held bus is fully visible, and add a short dashed stop line on the road at the timing post with the bus's nose touching it.

Also noted: the cream-on-cream moment midway through the 0→1 dolly, when the rider walks in front of the map. It lasts about 300 ms and is acceptable, but a fully clean version would route the walk behind the panel's lower edge.
