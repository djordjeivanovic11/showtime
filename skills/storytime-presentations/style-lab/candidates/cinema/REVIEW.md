# Cinema: honest self-review

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| First-glance story clarity | 4 | Ring + two lettered buses + film strip read instantly. "A → B" as a gap name still needs the speaker's first sentence. |
| World or template? | 5 | Every state is a lit night scene; chrome lives only in the letterbox bars; nothing could pass for a template slide. |
| Character warmth / usefulness | 3 | The rim-lit rider with amber scarf is recognisable and the poses read (watch, alarm/point, furled umbrella), but a silhouette has no face, so warmth is limited and the rider is small (~240 px) in the loop set-up. |
| Scaffold persistence | 5 | Route, both buses, gauge and rider are the same groups throughout; buses travel along the road, the splice slides, and exactly one thing changes per click within the loop. |
| Legibility at distance | 4 | Tag 60 px, labels ≥ 30 px, gauge values 40 px, reveal 200 px, all high-contrast. The rain texture over the title card and the dark overall key will suffer on a weak projector. |
| Polish / craft | 4 | Lighting, rim light, neon glow and the film-title reveal look intentional. The facades and the loop ground are simple, and the crowd is a little uniform. |
| Setting fit (keynote / TEDx / launch / gala) | 5 | Built for a dark room and a big screen; restrained, dramatic, legible. |
| One-shot reliability | 3 | The kit makes it repeatable, but getting it right took three rounds: arrow glyphs, neon layout, transition mud and reflection "stilts" are each easy to repeat. The recipe lists them. |
| PPTX feasibility | 3 | The chrome, gauge, labels and reveal export natively. Backgrounds and characters must be flattened PNGs, and curved bus motion and the staggered crane need workarounds. |

## Three weakest things in the final renders

1. **The rider is small and faceless in the loop states.** Posture carries the reaction,
   but from the back row the `watch` pose (wrist raised, head down) is subtle.
   *With more time:* add a lower-right inset "close-up" (a cinematic insert shot) of the
   rider for reaction beats, or push the camera in on the rider's stop in state 2.
2. **The top-left third of the loop set-up is empty sky** in states 1–2. It works as
   negative space, but it is unused real estate. *With more time:* frame the loop
   off-centre (rule of thirds) or let the crane end slightly lower, so the ring and the
   rider fill more of the picture.
3. **The fix hint is static.** `HOLD THE EARLY BUS` is a plate beside a stopped Bus B, but
   nothing shows the hold *working*. *With more time:* a tiny film strip on the plate
   showing the gaps re-levelling (19/1 → 10/10), or Bus B's front band lit with a pause
   glyph. Also the street-view buses can't show their letters at legible size.

Other honest notes: the gauge labels show intermediate whole minutes (13, 14…) while
the strip animates. That reads as a counter, but those values are not story numbers.
Rain is a decorative idle loop (it stops on `M`).
